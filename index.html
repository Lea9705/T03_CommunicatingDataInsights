Why energy labels exist

Australia's Energy Rating Label helps shoppers compare appliances at a glance. More stars generally means lower running costs. The figures above and on the Televisions page are drawn from the real GEMS register, not made-up numbers.

Televisions & energy use

Screen size is the single biggest driver of how much power a television draws. Cutting TV running costs

  1. Check the star rating. Star ratings in the GEMS data run from 1 up to 9. Even within the same screen-size band, the most and least efficient models differ enormously.
  2. Turn off standby. Standby power adds up over a year, even for a screen that's rarely switched fully off.
  3. Match size to room. Bigger isn't always better. A screen much larger than the room needs draws more power than necessary.
  4. Tune the picture settings. Lowering brightness from its out-of-the-box default can noticeably cut running cost.
